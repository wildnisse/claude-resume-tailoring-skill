# Personal Job Search Configuration

This file holds YOUR personal profile, preferences, and overrides. It is read at the start of every session. All default behavior, writing rules, quality gates, and pipeline mechanics live in the skill (`skill/SKILL.md` and `skill/STYLE.md`); anything you write here ADDS TO or OVERRIDES those defaults. Keep this file personal: if a rule would be good advice for any job seeker, it belongs in the skill, not here.

## User Profile

- **Name**: REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_NAME
- **Target Levels**: e.g. Senior Engineer / Engineering Manager / VP Engineering
- **Background**: 1–2 sentences about your career arc
- **Core Strengths**: 3–5 bullet points
- **Education**: highest degree and institution
- **Canonical figures**: years of experience, largest org led, headline metrics. These appear identically in every artifact; agents flag any drift.

## Personal Style Overrides

Defaults live in `skill/STYLE.md`. Add only what is specific to you:

- **Sign-off**: e.g. just your first name, no dash prefix, no "Best regards"
- Additional banned phrases or pet peeves beyond the skill defaults
- A reference letter or writing sample to match rhythm against, if you have one

## Pipeline Configuration

- **Master resume location**: `master-resumes/`
- **Master resume current version**: `v1` (update when you cut a new master)

## Targeting Strategy

What roles to pursue and avoid, informed by your actual funnel data. Examples:
- Levels and company sizes that convert for you
- Roles to treat as network-only plays
- Salary expectations that should be applied to scoring decisions

## Personal Notes

Free-form notes the skill should remember across sessions. Examples:
- Specific stories you've used in cover letters that worked well
- Confidential context about why you left previous roles
- Companies you've decided to no longer pursue and why
- How to present unusual entries (contract titles, parallel ventures, anonymized clients)
